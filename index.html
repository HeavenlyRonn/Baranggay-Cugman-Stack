About Us

Barangay Cugman Management System helps residents request documents.

The Barangay Cugman Online Request System is a digital platform designed to streamline and simplify how residents acquire essential local documents. Through this secure online portal, residents can make requests for Barangay Clearance, Indigency, Residency, and other certificates with ease and transparency. Our mission is to make community services easily accessible for every Cugman resident.
